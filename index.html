Timeline:

1960 - 1978: LASCO FC

Bandari FC traces its roots to LASCO FC, founded in 1960, which marked the beginning of organized football linked to Mombasa's port community.

1978 - 1985: Cargo FC

The club transitioned into Cargo FC in 1978, strengthening its football identity in the coastal region.

1985: Formation of Bandari FC

In 1985, Cargo FC merged with the Kenya Ports Authority (KPA) football team to form Bandari FC, symbolizing its connection to the Port of Mombasa.

1999: Club Disbandment

Following relegation and challenges, the club was temporarily disbanded in 1999.

2004: Club Revival

Bandari FC was revived in 2004 by KPA and began rebuilding its place in Kenyan football.

2015 & 2019: Major Success

The club won the FKF Shield Cup in 2015 and 2019, also lifting the Kenya Super Cup in 2019 and representing Kenya in the CAF Confederation Cup.

MBARAKI STADIUM

Mbaraki Stadium is the proud home of Bandari FC, located in the coastal city of Mombasa, Kenya. Established to strengthen football development in the coastal region, the stadium was envisioned in 2014 and officially opened in 2021 after years of planning and construction. Inspired by Mombasa’s rich maritime heritage, the stadium symbolizes the ambition and growth of football on the Kenyan coast. Today, Mbaraki Stadium hosts Bandari FC matches, youth tournaments, and community sporting events, creating an unforgettable matchday atmosphere for fans.
